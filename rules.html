Role Around the World: rules and guide

You start a campaign in Trafalgar Square with £350. Each leg takes you to a random city 1,000 to 2,000 km away. Your aim is to arrive on time, keep your money growing, and enjoy the journey.

Planning

When a leg starts you see the destination's name. Tap the map to drop a pin where you think the city is, then lock in your guess. The further off you are, the more time you lose before you set off.

Choosing a route

You're offered a few routes: fast, cheap, and one through interesting places. Each has a description, and the costs and times appear once you set off. The target time is shown, and is based on the reasonable routes.

Travelling

Choose where to go next from the options at each stop. Trains, buses and ferries leave at set times. Some services run overnight, and you may need a hotel. Looking for routes costs time but reveals more options. You can switch between stations, bus stations, ferry terminals and airports.

Target and bonus

Arrive by the target time, counting planning, travel and the last mile, and you earn a £150 bonus. Money you keep carries over into the next leg.

Last mile

At the destination you find the exact spot from a photo, using the map. Your distance off costs time: nothing within 100 m, then about a minute for every 10 m beyond that.

Interim photo stops

At culture-rich cities along the way you can stop for photo work. It costs two hours. You earn between £50 and £100 depending on how close your pin is, and each city pays once per campaign.

Income

Each leg starts with £350. Keep your money growing and you can keep going: the campaign is open-ended.
