Role Around the World: rules and guide

You start a campaign in Trafalgar Square with £350. Each leg takes you to a random city 1,000 to 2,000 km away. Explore at your own pace, manage your money, and keep a journal of your travels.

Planning

When a leg starts you see the destination's name. Tap the map to drop a pin where you think the city is, then lock in your guess. The further off you are, the more time you lose before you set off.

Choosing a route

A short overview introduces the kinds of connections available for your journey. Then choose individual services as you go; fares and departure times are shown at each stop. You can switch between stations, bus stations, ferry terminals and airports. The target time is based on reasonable journeys and earns you a bonus if you arrive in time.

Travelling

Choose where to go next from the options at each stop. Trains, buses and ferries leave at set times, shown on each option as the day and time you leave and arrive. Looking for routes costs time but reveals more options.

Overnight travel

If you have to wait more than four hours and the wait covers the night (10 pm to 6 am), you need somewhere to sleep. Pick a room in town, or spend the night at the station, which is free but leaves you tired and costs an hour on the next leg. Some long-distance buses and trains run through the night. If you take one, you sleep on board and no hotel is needed. Shared minibuses and taxis leave when full, so they never need a night's stay.

Service hours

Services leave only within their operating hours, and a service that is due outside those hours waits until it reopens.

Target and bonus

Arrive by the target time, counting planning, travel and the last mile, and you earn a £150 bonus. Money you keep carries over into the next leg.

Last mile

At the destination you find the exact spot from a photo, using the map. Your distance off costs time: nothing within 100 m, then about a minute for every 10 m beyond that.

Interim photo stops

At culture-rich cities along the way you can stop for photo work. It costs two hours. You earn between £50 and £100 depending on how close your pin is, and each city pays once per campaign.

Income

At the start of each new leg you receive £350, paid when you set off at 8 am on the morning after your rest day. It is added to the money you carry over. Keep your money growing and you can keep going: the campaign is open-ended. On a rest day, you can visit the local market, buy goods and find buyers in later towns; trading is optional.
